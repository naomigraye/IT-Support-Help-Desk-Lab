# Ticket 004 — Employee Offboarding

## Ticket Summary
HR requested that a simulated former employee's company access be disabled after the employee left the organization.

## Environment
- Microsoft Azure
- Microsoft Entra ID
- Security Groups
- Identity & Access Management

## Request
Disable the former employee's account and remove access to company resources.

## Resolution Steps
1. Located the user's account in Microsoft Entra ID.
2. Disabled the user's ability to sign in.
3. Removed the user from the Sales security group.
4. Revoked the user's active sign-in sessions.
5. Verified that the account was disabled and department access was removed.

## Resolution
The user's account was successfully disabled, active sessions were revoked, and Sales department access was removed.

- Employee Offboarding
- Account Deactivation
- Session Revocation
- Security Group Management
- Identity & Access Management (IAM)
